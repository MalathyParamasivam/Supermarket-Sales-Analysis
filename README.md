# 🛒 Supermarket Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

The Supermarket Sales Analysis project focuses on analyzing supermarket transaction data to understand sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product preferences, and payment patterns.

Python was used for data cleaning, preprocessing, and Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), while Power BI was used to create an interactive dashboard to visualize key business insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall supermarket sales performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Identify top-performing and low-performing product lines.
- Compare sales performance across branches.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Understand customer payment preferences.
- Generate actionable business insights and recommendations.

## 📂 Dataset Information

- **Dataset:** Supermarket Sales Dataset
- **Total Records:** 1,000
- **Time Period:** January – March
- **Domain:** Retail / Supermarket

### Dataset Features

- Branch and City
- Customer Type and Gender
- Product Line
- Unit Price and Quantity
- Tax and Total Sales
- Payment Method
- Date and Time
- Rating and Gross Income

## 🔍 Project Workflow

1. **Data Loading:** Imported the supermarket sales dataset into Python.
2. **Data Understanding:** Examined the dataset structure, columns, data types, and statistical summaries.
3. **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:** Checked missing values and duplicates, converted Date and Time columns into appropriate data types, and extracted Month from the Date column.
4. **Outlier Detection:** Used boxplots to identify potential outliers and retained genuine values that were not significantly affecting the analysis.
5. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Performed univariate and bivariate analysis to understand distributions, comparisons, and relationships.
6. **Data Visualization:** Used histograms, boxplots, barplots, countplots, and pairplots to explore patterns.
7. **Power BI Dashboard:** Developed an interactive dashboard to present key sales and customer insights.

## 📊 Key Business Insights

- Food and Beverages recorded the highest sales among product lines.
- Health and Beauty recorded the lowest sales.
- January recorded the highest monthly sales, followed by March and February.
- Branch C had the highest average transaction value among the three branches.
- E-wallet was the most preferred payment method, followed by Cash and Credit Card.
- Member and Normal customers contributed almost equally to transactions.
- Food and Beverages and Fashion Accessories received the highest average customer ratings.
- Home and Lifestyle recorded the lowest average customer rating.
- Purchasing patterns varied across product lines and customer genders.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Focus on improving sales in low-performing product categories.
- Use customer purchasing patterns to plan targeted marketing campaigns.
- Maintain sufficient stock for high-demand product lines.
- Investigate the reasons behind lower monthly sales.
- Encourage customer membership through suitable loyalty programs.
- Review customer feedback to improve satisfaction in lower-rated categories.
- Maintain convenient and reliable e-wallet payment facilities.
